Earth rendering credits

Surface BRDF adapted from Solaris by Cuvii:
https://github.com/thecuvii/solaris/blob/5e2e15e3db18001a764fcef23f60cbc2d59ae5ef/src/earth/atmospheric-orb.effect.tsx

CodeSesh uses Solaris surface lighting and textures in its existing WebGL sphere renderer.
The full Solaris atmospheric scattering and bloom pipeline is not included.

Earth textures: Solar System Scope (INOVE), CC BY 4.0.
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The six WebP maps were prepared by Cuvii from the three.js Earth example textures
at commit 444f238c63b594fbaf1d5adde301fa7e10c29a83:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/444f238c63b594fbaf1d5adde301fa7e10c29a83/examples/textures/planets
Cuvii converted day/night maps, extracted cloud and roughness channels,
generated a tangent-space normal map, and derived the ocean material mask.
CodeSesh resized these maps from 2048x1024 to 512x256 using cwebp (quality 85)
for the small sphere in the landing page solar system.

Source and adaptation details:
https://github.com/thecuvii/solaris/blob/5e2e15e3db18001a764fcef23f60cbc2d59ae5ef/website/public/textures/v1/CREDITS.md
